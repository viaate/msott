House rules for the holidays

  • Gifts with food in them stay off the floor. Wrapped chocolate under the tree is easy for a dog to find. We can smell it through the paper.
  • Anchor the tree and keep breakable ornaments and tinsel up high, out of tail range.
  • Cords and batteries: tuck light cords away and keep button batteries locked up. Chewing either is an emergency.
  • Visitors: tell guests not to share. A quiet room with water, a bed and a chew helps an overwhelmed dog.
  • New Year's fireworks coming? My calm corner has tips for scared dogs.

I'm a dog, not a vet. This is general info. Every dog is different (size, health, allergies), so check with your vet.
