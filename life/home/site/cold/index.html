❄️ Too Cold for a Walk?

Hi, I'm Charlie, an 18 lb doodle with opinions about cold paws. Tell me about your dog and the weather and I'll give you a rough idea of how long a walk stays comfy.

Winter walk tips from a dog
  • Wipe paws after walks: road salt and de-icer sting and aren't for licking. Booties or paw balm help.
  • Antifreeze tastes sweet and is very poisonous. Keep us away from puddles in driveways.
  • Shorter, more frequent walks beat one long freezing one. Indoor games count too (find-the-treat!).
  • Shivering, lifting paws, slowing down, whining, or trying to turn home = time to go in.
  • Snowballs in curly fur are real. A quick trim between paw pads helps.
This is a rough, friendly guide, not veterinary advice. Every dog is different. If your dog seems very cold, lethargic, or confused, warm them up and call your vet.
