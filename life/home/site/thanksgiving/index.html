A few house rules for the big day

  • Plain means plain. No butter, salt, gravy, onion, garlic or spices. Little bits only: treats should be a small part of what your dog eats in a day.
  • Guard the trash. Turkey carcasses, strings and foil in the bin are a classic emergency.
  • Tell your guests. The kind uncle sneaking stuffing under the table means well.
  • A quiet room with water, a bed and a chew helps a lot if your dog gets overwhelmed by visitors.

I'm a dog, not a vet. This is general info. Every dog is different (health conditions, allergies, size), so check with your vet. If your dog ate something worrying, call your vet or an emergency line right away: ASPCA Animal Poison Control (US) (888) 426-4435 or Pet Poison Helpline (855) 764-7661 (fees may apply).
